# In-store purchases and catalogue review

## Application changes

Authorized staff can record a paid shop bill from **Admin → Orders → In-store Purchase Entry**. The form accepts an existing customer ID or new customer details, a unique bill number, purchase date and final paid amount. It creates a paid, delivered order without product entry or stock deduction and reuses the existing referral, wallet and reseller commission completion rules.

Bill numbers are normalized and protected by a database unique constraint. Customer creation, purchase, external payment record, audit events and rewards commit together. Purchase history and sales reporting use the bill date; audit records retain the recording time. The entry uses existing global referral rules because no product categories are available.

Apply the `orders.0007` and `customer_services.0010` migrations to the existing persistent database before releasing this code. See `IN_STORE_PURCHASES.md` for API fields and operating details.
